Measure a self-hosted font for its fallback faces with the `measure-font` bin and `@adrienlcp/styles/font-metrics`: the zero's advance in em at any weight, to convert a `ch`; each `$widths` ratio of `fonts.fallback-faces` — the band's Arial cut scaled by `size-adjust`, over the web font at that weight —, fontaine's `size-adjust`, and the capital height, read from the `H` when the `OS/2` table has none. `fonts.fallback-faces` takes `$style: italic`, drawn in Arial Italic and Arial Bold Italic, which `metricTwins()` now widens to their twins. The audit lists a font token that names a web font without its `'<family> fallback'` face — fontaine never adds it inside a custom property — with `findFallbackFailures` and `webFontFamilies`.
